DocuMind AI: Delete Your Account

This page explains how to delete your DocuMind AI account and the data linked to it. App name: DocuMind AI.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the DocuMind AI app and sign in with Google.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when asked.

What gets deleted

  • Your account information (name and email address from Google Sign-In)
  • All documents you uploaded
  • All data extracted from or generated for your documents (summaries, answers, chat history)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your data is removed from our systems within 7 days of the request.

Data we may retain

We do not keep your personal data after deletion, except for limited technical logs that may be retained for a short period for security and legal compliance.

Can't access the app?

If you cannot open the app, email us from your registered Google email address and we will delete your account and data for you:
