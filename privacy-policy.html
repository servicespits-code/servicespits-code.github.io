1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Prerna Info Tech Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use the Prerna Info Tech Solutions mobile application (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account details (e.g. name, email address, password) when you register
  • Profile information you choose to add
  • Content you create, upload, or submit within the App
  • Communications when you contact us for support

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (model, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (features used, screens viewed, time spent, crash logs)
  • Approximate location, if you grant location permission
  • IP address and general network information

2.3 Information From Third Parties

  • Sign-in providers (e.g. Sign in with Apple, Google) if you use them
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers
  • Payment processors, for purchase confirmation (we do not store full card details)

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Create and manage your account
  • Personalize your experience and content
  • Process transactions and send related confirmations
  • Send important updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Monitor usage, diagnose problems, and improve performance
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who perform functions on our behalf (hosting, analytics, customer support, payment processing)
  • Legal authorities, when required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights and safety
  • Business transfers, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With your consent, for any other purpose disclosed at the time of collection

5. Data Storage & Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures — such as encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymize the data.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Disable location, camera, or notification permissions via device Settings

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. Residents of the EU/EEA, UK, and applicable US states may have additional rights under laws such as GDPR and state privacy statutes.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, and processed in, countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect information transferred internationally.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy in the App or on this page and updating the "Last updated" date above.
